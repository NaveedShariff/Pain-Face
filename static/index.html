Methods

  1. GREEN: Verkruysse, Svaasand & Nelson. Remote plethysmographic imaging using ambient light. Opt. Express, 2008.
  2. ICA: Poh, McDuff & Picard. Non-contact, automated cardiac pulse measurements using video imaging and blind source separation. Opt. Express, 2010.
  3. PCA: Lewandowska et al. Measuring pulse rate with a webcam. FedCSIS, 2011.
  4. CHROM: de Haan & Jeanne. Robust pulse rate from chrominance-based rPPG. IEEE TBME, 2013.
  5. PBV: de Haan & van Leest. Improved motion robustness of remote-PPG by using the blood volume pulse signature. Physiol. Meas., 2014.
  6. POS: Wang, den Brinker, Stuijk & de Haan. Algorithmic principles of remote PPG. IEEE TBME, 2017.
  7. LGI: Pilz et al. Local group invariance for heart rate estimation from face videos. CVPR-W, 2018.
  8. OMIT: Casado & López. Face2PPG: an unsupervised pipeline for blood volume pulse extraction from faces. 2023.
  9. Detrending: Tarvainen, Ranta-aho & Karjalainen. An advanced detrending method with application to HRV analysis. IEEE TBME, 2002.
  10. Face mesh: MediaPipe Face Landmarker (Apache-2.0). Deep models: open-rppg (FacePhys, PhysMamba, PhysFormer, TS-CAN, EfficientPhys, PhysNet).

Research tool only. It is not a medical device and its readings must not be used for diagnosis or treatment. The SpO₂ ratio is an uncalibrated index, not an oxygen saturation.
