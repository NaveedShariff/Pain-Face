Pain-Face — facial action units via Py-Feat (Detectorv2: 20 AUs, 7 emotions, 68 landmarks, head pose, gaze, 52 blendshapes), pulse and autonomic metrics via the 8-method rPPG bank, fused into a PSPI-anchored pain index.
Research instrument only. Not a medical device — it estimates observable pain behaviour, not what a person feels, and must not be used for diagnosis, triage or treatment.
